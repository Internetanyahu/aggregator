Source and verification rules

Each result links to its publisher and retains its publication date and retrieval time. Search results can be stale, duplicated, irrelevant, or incomplete. Open the original source and independently confirm the property, availability, authority to sell, ownership and terms. No private homeowner contact details are harvested. No motivation or distress score is inferred. Imported items enter your local seller register as unverified leads; data remains in this browser.
